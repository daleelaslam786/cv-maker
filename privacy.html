Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CV Maker ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses and protects information from users ("you", "your") of the CV Maker iOS app.

Overview

CV Maker helps you write CVs and cover letters. Everything you write or import stays on your device. There is no sign-up or account.

Your CVs stay on your device

Your CVs, cover letters, photo and the job ads you paste are stored only on your iPhone. AI features run on your iPhone using Apple's on-device model. We have no server and never receive your CV content.

Information we collect

  • Usage analytics (Google Firebase Analytics): which screens you open, which features you use, and in-app purchase events (product and price). It also collects an app-instance identifier and an approximate location (country or region, from your IP address). Events never include anything you type.
  • Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics): technical details when the app crashes, such as device model and iOS version, so we can fix it.

None of this is linked to your identity.

Photos

If you add a photo to your CV, you pick it with the iOS photo picker. Only that photo is used, and it stays on your device.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Advertising and tracking

The app does not show ads, does not use your device's advertising identifier, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Your choices

  • Turn usage data off any time in the app's Settings → Share anonymous usage data. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland it is off until you turn it on.
  • Delete every CV, cover letter and photo in Settings → Delete all my data, or by deleting the app.

Data retention

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days, then deleted by Google.

Third-party services

Google Firebase: policies.google.com/privacy

Children's privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

GDPR and CCPA

You can ask us about, or ask us to delete, any data relating to you by emailing us. Because we hold no accounts and no CV content, there is usually nothing on our side to delete beyond anonymous analytics.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page, through the Privacy Policy link on our App Store page and in the app.
